# CBPayments Blog — 100-Article Editorial Project

**Status:** Drafting / review only. Nothing in this project is approved for public publication.  
**Repository:** https://github.com/cbpayments/cbpayments-blog  
**Review branch:** `content-review-100-articles`  
**Public site:** https://blog.cbpayments.cc/

## Scope
- 50 distinct editorial topics.
- Each topic receives one Chinese article and one independently localized English article: 100 drafts total.
- Drafts are stored separately from the live site. No draft is added to the public homepage, archive, sitemap, or production branch before approval.
- After editorial approval, publication will be handled as a separate release step.

## Editorial rules
1. Explain concepts and user tasks in practical language; avoid keyword stuffing, spun translations, filler and repetitive introductions.
2. Chinese and English versions must be independently written for their audiences, not literal sentence-by-sentence translations.
3. Never invent CBPayments product capabilities, supported countries, issuing partners, card limits, top-up methods, fees, exchange rates, KYC requirements, settlement times, refund rights, licensing or regulatory status.
4. Any product-specific step that cannot be verified against current official product documentation must be marked **[需产品确认] / [Product confirmation required]** and must not be presented as fact.
5. Do not claim “zero card issuance fee”, “zero fees”, guaranteed approval, universal acceptance, guaranteed security, guaranteed returns or guaranteed transaction success.
6. Explain material risks: network mismatch, irreversible transfers, volatility, stablecoin/issuer risk, fraud, phishing, account security, chargebacks and jurisdiction-specific restrictions where relevant.
7. Each article includes: H1, short introduction, useful H2/H3 structure, actionable detail, FAQs where appropriate, concise conclusion, SEO title, meta description, primary keyword, secondary keywords, slug, category, internal-link suggestions and structured-data notes.
8. Use neutral educational disclosures. Product references should link naturally to https://cbpayments.cc/ only where relevant.
9. Review factual claims, originality, language quality, SEO metadata, internal links, canonical URL and schema before approval.
10. Drafts are not legal, financial, tax or investment advice.
