OUR STANDARDS

Editorial Policy

The principles we follow when preparing and maintaining educational content.

Purpose and scope

The CBPayments Blog publishes general educational content about digital payments, crypto assets, blockchain networks and related technology. It is not a substitute for professional financial, legal, tax or compliance advice.

Research and accuracy

We aim to explain concepts in plain language, distinguish established facts from opinions and avoid unsupported statistics or promises. Where details may change, such as fees, supported networks, regulations or product availability, readers should verify information with the relevant provider and official sources before acting.

Human review before publication

Draft articles are prepared for editorial review before they are published. A draft is not considered approved merely because it has been written or uploaded to a private working area. The site owner must approve the final text and target URL before publication to the public website.

Product references and conflicts

CBPayments may be mentioned where relevant to the topic. Product references should be clearly distinguishable from general educational explanations. We do not promise that any service is available in every country or suitable for every reader.

Updates and corrections

We may update articles when information changes or an error is identified. Material updates should be reflected in the page's modified date and, where appropriate, in the XML sitemap. Readers may use the contact channels published on the CBPayments website to report a possible error.

Security and financial-risk content

Articles should encourage readers to verify wallet addresses, networks, fees and service terms, and should not request private keys, recovery phrases or other authentication secrets.
